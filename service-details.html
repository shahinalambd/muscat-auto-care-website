Full Car Detailing

Inside and out, panel by panel. Decontamination, polish, protection and a complete interior clean.

About this service

Our most complete treatment. The paint is washed, decontaminated and machine polished to bring back depth and gloss, then protected. Inside, the cabin receives a full deep clean. It is the service to book when you want the car to look and feel close to new again.

Benefits

  • Restores gloss and colour depth
  • Removes light swirls and oxidation
  • Interior and exterior done in one visit
  • Lasting protection against sun and dust

What's included

  • Hand wash and decontamination
  • Clay bar treatment
  • Single-stage machine polish
  • Paint sealant
  • Full interior deep clean
  • Engine bay wipe-down

Good to know

  • Suitable forCars that need a reset, special occasions and pre-sale preparation.
  • Typical durationFull day

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking ahead guarantees your time slot, especially for detailing and polishing, which take several hours. For a quick exterior wash, walk-ins can be accommodated when a bay is free.

How long will my car be with you?

It depends on the service. An exterior wash takes around an hour, interior cleaning two to three hours, and a full detail usually takes a full day. We confirm timing when you book.

Which products do you use?

We use pH-balanced shampoos, dedicated wheel cleaners, leather-safe interior products and professional polishes and sealants chosen for each surface. Ask us if you would like product details for your car.
