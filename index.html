Signature treatment

Complete car detailing

Our most complete treatment. The paint is washed, decontaminated and machine polished to bring back depth and gloss, then protected. Inside, the cabin receives a full deep clean. It is the service to book when you want the car to look and feel close to new again.

  • Decontamination and clay barBonded dirt removed so polish works on clean paint.
  • Machine polishLight swirls and haze reduced for deeper gloss.
  • Sealant protectionA durable layer against sun, dust and water spots.
  • Full interior cleanSeats, carpets, dashboard and vents, done by hand.
